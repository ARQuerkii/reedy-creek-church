MINISTRIES

FIND WHERE YOU FIT

Faith at Reedy Creek isn't just something that happens on Saturday morning. Our ministries are how we live it out the rest of the week - serving our community, supporting families, and growing together.

For Families Youth & Young Adults Get Involved Faith & Worship

FOR FAMILIES

FAMILIES

Structured programs that give kids a place of their own - Biblical values, practical skills and genuine friendships, from kindergarten through high school.

Adventurers

Ages 4-9 · Contact us for current schedule

A weekly club for younger kids, blending crafts, games, community service and Bible-based values with a simple honours system - similar in spirit to Scouts, with a spiritual foundation underneath. A uniform is part of the program.

Contact the Pastor to Enrol →

Pathfinders

Ages 10-15 · Contact us for current schedule

Camping, community service, leadership development and a badge-based honours system, run as part of the wider Pathfinders movement recognised across the South Pacific Division - not just an informal kids' group.

Learn More →

Children's Sabbath School

9:30am, Saturday

Beginners, Primary, Juniors and Teens each have their own age-appropriate class every Saturday at 9:30am, at the same time as the adult Sabbath School - so the whole family arrives and settles in together.

See Sabbath School →

YOUTH & YOUNG ADULTS

YOUTH & YOUNG ADULTS

We don't run a separate teen program - from 16 up, this is where young people connect, both on Saturday mornings and throughout the week.

Young Adults (YYA)

Ages 16+ · Regular events - contact us for the current calendar

Retreats, camps, hikes and run club, community outreach - including regular homeless outreach feeds with The Movement - plus bonfires, rallies and social nights throughout the year. If you're 16 or older, this is your crew.

Get In Touch →

Young Adults Sabbath School

9:30am, Saturday

Our young adult Bible study group, running every Saturday at 9:30am - same Sabbath, same time, different room and a more discussion-based style. We study using Inverse, the Bible study guide made for young adults.

Find Out More →

GET INVOLVED

GET INVOLVED

For people who want to serve, contribute or connect beyond just attending.

Personal Ministries (Community Services)

Ongoing

Our practical, outward-facing arm - community morning teas, food pantry support, and outreach programs like The Movement, which runs homeless outreach feeds. This is how we show up for people outside our own walls.

Get In Touch →

Music Ministry

Every Saturday, 10:45am service

Worship music leans traditional but isn't limited to hymns, led by piano, strings and other orchestral instruments rather than a full band. If you play an instrument or love to sing, there's room for you.

Get In Touch to Play or Sing →

Prayer Ministry

Every night, via Zoom

A structured group of intercessors who meet every night on Zoom. Reach out to Sue to join the group, or simply to send through a prayer request - no need to attend in person.

Message Sue →

Social Committee

Regular events throughout the year

The team behind our social calendar - international dinner nights, bubble tea walks, sports and games nights, and whatever else keeps the congregation connecting outside of church hours. What's coming up gets posted to our Instagram.
