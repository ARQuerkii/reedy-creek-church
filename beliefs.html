OUR FAITH

WHAT WE BELIEVE

Here's the plain-language version of what we hold to be true - no theology degree required. If you want the complete picture, there's a link to our full statement of beliefs further down the page.

Rooted in the Bible

We're a Christian church, and everything we do starts with Scripture. If we can't point to it in the Bible, we hold it loosely.

Saturday Worship

We worship on Saturday - the biblical seventh-day Sabbath, running sundown Friday to sundown Saturday - as a day set apart for rest and community.

Whole-Person Health

We believe physical, mental and spiritual health are connected - which is part of why health and lifestyle show up so often in what we do.

A Global Family

We're part of a worldwide denomination of around 22 million people across 200+ countries - so wherever you travel, there's likely a familiar Sabbath waiting.

Baptism by Immersion

Baptism is a personal, public declaration of faith in Jesus - not something inherited, and not something rushed. It's a decision made when someone is ready.

Living with Hope

We believe Jesus is coming back, and that shapes how we live now - less about predicting dates, more about living like it matters today.
