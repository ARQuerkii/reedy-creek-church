ABOUT US

WHO WE ARE

Who we are, where we've come from, and what we care about.

GET TO KNOW US

Reedy Creek Adventist Church is a small, close-knit community on the Gold Coast - the kind of place where people notice if you weren't there last Saturday. We're Seventh-day Adventist, but our congregation holds people at every stage of belief, from lifelong believers to those still quietly figuring things out. Sabbath brings us back together each week, and there's always a shared lunch afterwards. That's where a lot of the real conversations happen.

OUR STORY

HOW WE GOT HERE

Our church has been part of the Reedy Creek and Gold Coast community since 1983 - more than four decades. Over that time we've grown from a small group of families into a congregation of around 100 people, and we still feel like a family.

Every Saturday night at 6:30, a team from our church takes the LifeBoat food van down to Burleigh Beach. We bring homemade soup and bread from local bakeries for people sleeping rough. It's all part of our Lighthouse initiative - and there's a large lighthouse standing in one of our church rooms as a reminder of why we keep showing up. No announcement, no fanfare; it just happens, week after week.

PART OF SOMETHING BIGGER

OUR CONNECTION TO THE WIDER CHURCH

We're part of the Seventh-day Adventist Church - a global Christian denomination founded in the 1860s and now active in over 200 countries. In Australia, we're connected through the South Queensland Conference and the Adventist Church Australia network. That means when you visit us, you're connecting with a local community that's also part of something much larger.
