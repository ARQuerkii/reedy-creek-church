GIVING

WHERE YOUR GIFT GOES

An honest look at how giving works in the Seventh-day Adventist Church - from our church here at Reedy Creek to the mission around the world.

NEVER EXPECTED

It's always your choice

No one is ever asked what they give, and visitors are never expected to give at all. Come, worship and stay for lunch - that's all we hope for.

ALWAYS NEEDED

But it keeps us going

Our church runs on the generosity of its people. Giving keeps the lights on and the doors open, and it funds the pastors, programs and outreach that make Reedy Creek what it is.

THE BASICS

Two ways to give

Adventists give in two ways, and each one does a different job.

Tithe

A tenth, returned to God

Many Adventists return a tenth of their income as tithe. Our church doesn't keep any of it. Every dollar of tithe goes to our local conference, which uses it mainly to pay pastors.

Offerings

Any amount, for a set purpose

Offerings are freewill gifts on top of tithe. Each Sabbath's offering has a set purpose - often our own local church budget, and sometimes world mission or disaster relief.

TITHE

Where tithe goes

Most tithe pays for ministry - the pastors, chaplains and Bible workers who serve local churches. Hover over the chart or the list to see each part.

ONE CHURCH, WORLDWIDE

How tithe travels

Tithe is shared so that small churches and mission areas are supported, not just the places that can afford it.

Reedy Creek Church

Collects your tithe and sends all of it on.

South Queensland Conference

Pays our pastors and supports churches across South Queensland.

Australian Union Conference

Supports the church's work across Australia.

South Pacific Division

Supports the church across the Pacific, including island missions.

General Conference

Funds the church's mission around the world.

OFFERINGS

Where offerings go

Each offering goes exactly where it says. These are the three you'll hear about most.

Stays at Reedy Creek

Local Church Budget

Every dollar stays right here, invested in our church home and our community. It covers:

  • Utilities and building maintenance
  • Worship and ministry resources
  • Sabbath School and outreach programs
  • Community care and local mission
Around the world

World Mission

Sabbath School mission offerings fund Adventist mission around the world - since 1913, every cent of them. Each quarter's 13th Sabbath offering is split like this:

25% special projects named in that quarter's lesson 75% the world mission budget
Disaster & relief

ADRA

ADRA is the Adventist Church's humanitarian agency. Disaster and Famine Relief offerings fund ADRA Australia's response when disaster strikes, and the yearly ADRA Appeal supports its relief and development work.
