GIVING

WHERE YOUR GIFT GOES

How giving works at Reedy Creek, and the ministries it keeps going.

NEVER EXPECTED

It's always your choice

No one is ever asked what they give, and visitors are never expected to give at all. Come, worship and stay for lunch - that's all we hope for.

ALWAYS NEEDED

But it keeps us going

Our church runs on the generosity of its people. Giving keeps the lights on and the doors open, and it funds the pastors, programs and outreach that make Reedy Creek what it is.

Tithes & offerings

Tithes and offerings are gratefully accepted.

EVERY MINISTRY

Shared across our ministries

Your gift supports every ministry at Reedy Creek. It goes where it's needed most at the time - whichever ministry needs a little more love.

Kids & Families

Adventurers, Pathfinders and Sabbath School.

Young Adults

Camps, hikes and Bible study for ages 16 and up.

Community Outreach

The LifeBoat food van and The Movement.

Health & Wellness

Wellness courses all year round with ELIA Wellness.

Worship & Fellowship

Music, media, prayer, men's catch-ups and social nights.

Sabbath School

Bible studies and discussions for every age, Saturdays at 9:30am.

OFFERINGS

Where offerings go

Each offering goes exactly where it says. These are the three you'll hear about most.

Stays at Reedy Creek

Local Church Budget

Every dollar stays right here, invested in our church home and our community. It covers:

  • Utilities and building maintenance
  • Worship and ministry resources
  • Sabbath School and outreach programs
  • Community care and local mission
Around the world

World Mission

Sabbath School mission offerings fund Adventist mission work around the world, including special projects featured in each quarter's lesson.

Disaster & relief

ADRA

ADRA is the Adventist Church's humanitarian agency. Disaster and Famine Relief offerings fund ADRA Australia's response when disaster strikes, and the yearly ADRA Appeal supports its relief and development work.

  • Visit ADRA Australia
